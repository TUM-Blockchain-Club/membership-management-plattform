# Member card portraits

The member directory uses horizontal cards with a large circular portrait centered vertically in the left 34% and member details on the right. Images keep their proportions with object-cover; the original upload remains unchanged. Missing images use initials.

Contact actions (email and five social platforms) align to the bottom right and wrap on narrow screens. The existing permission-gated Edit / My Profile action sits above them. Cards use a minimum preferred width of 360px, bounded by the viewport, so the grid adapts without squeezing portraits and contact actions.

Verified in the browser at 320, 390, 768, 1024, and 1600 pixels, including a member with all six contact actions and opening the edit dialog.
